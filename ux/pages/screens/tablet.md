## Tablet

| | |
|--|--|
| **Purpose** | Timeline-first with peek inspector; side inspector when wide enough |
| **Layout** | Header with the Stage bar · headers + timeline · Player bar. Ordinary modifier sheets dock above the Player bar and scroll their heading, actions, fields, errors, and audition footer together. Maximize timeline hides the tabs and docks the sheet above the status row. The compact inspector docks above the status row with a pinned header and one bounded body scroller for feedback and fields. |
| **Playhead** | Moving (desktop-like), not the phone playhead that holds the center once passed |
| **Out of scope** | Phone bottom tab bar as primary IA |

```
┌─ Header · Stage bar ────────────────────┐
│ Headers │ Timeline    ┌ peek inspector ┐│
├─────────┴─────────────┴────────────────┤
│ Player bar                             │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
