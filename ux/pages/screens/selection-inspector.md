## Shared: Selection → Inspector / Bottom sheet

| | |
|--|--|
| **Purpose** | Modifier for whatever is selected (pending cut, clip fade, track FX, word, …) |
| **Primary actions** | Cut / Keep on the Action bar (Leave / Reject for a suggestion to leave alone; Approve / Reject there for a split, exact range or no accept action) / Restore · Hear before / Hear after (footer Play around where there is no Action bar) · param edits · Clear selection (the last button of the Action bar for a clip, seam, track, range, marker or comment; it drops the selection and the Inspector with it, and a selection of words keeps the Script header's Clear instead) · Close (hides the Inspector and keeps the selection) |
| **Data shown** | Type badge · label · agent reason · source + timeline ranges · shape-specific params |
| **Phone** | Ordinary modifier sheets use half or full height. Heading, actions, fields, errors, and audition footer scroll together. Scroll to the top for Expand and Collapse; Close is in the header at half and full height, and at the dock detent it sits at the end of the dock, where it hides the sheet and keeps the selection (dragging the sheet down past the dock is what clears it). The dock holds at most five verb slots; an end control (Clear, or Close in the Inspector dock) takes one, so a range, whose scope chip takes a slot too, keeps Hear and Split on the dock and moves Comment and Record to More, and at 360 px or narrower the object shows its icon only. Ordinary modifiers use a taller half height. The compact inspector uses peek, half, and full heights with its header pinned outside one bounded body scroller. Feedback and fields scroll in that body. If the full sheet leaves too little room for one field below a long heading, the Inspector opens full-screen. A clip's trim and fade fields, a suggestion's Source start and end, and an envelope point's Time and Level each carry the strip's fine and coarse minus and plus nudge buttons (hold to repeat) beside the field. Sheets never stack. |
| **Tablet** | The Inspector card anchors to the floating Action bar in the Edit workspace, with the selected element or workspace bottom edge as fallback. Its heading stays fixed while its body scrolls. A pending edit's verbs are on the Action bar, so the card clears that bar and nothing else. If there is room for both, the card stays clear of the Timeline view controls, including Zoom and Script. The floating Action bar also stays clear of those controls, and the card slides beside the bar, or shortens to the room above or below it, when its minimum height would reach into it. |
| **Desktop** | The Inspector card anchors to the floating Action bar in the Edit workspace, with the selected element or workspace bottom edge as fallback. Its heading stays fixed while its body scrolls. A pending edit's verbs are on the Action bar, so the card clears that bar and nothing else. |
| **Keyboard** | On desktop and tablet, press **I** after panning a selected Timeline clip offscreen. The Timeline scrolls back to the item and focus moves into the Inspector. The playhead stays in place. |
| **Focus** | Close returns focus to the control that opened the Inspector, including a phone sheet opened from a track header. Focus you moved elsewhere before Close stays where it is. The dock Close at the dock detent returns focus the same way. Clear selection from the Action bar puts focus on the clip or seam that was selected, or on the stage when that is gone. |
| **Empty** | “Select a clip, edit, or word” |
| **Out of scope** | Deep nested wizards; one panel per MCP tool name |

A fade gesture based on values changed by Undo or another editor saves nothing.
The current project shows "This clip changed. Nothing was saved. Adjust the fade
again." Repeat the gesture against the restored values. The inspector shows the
error inline; timeline handles and phone nudges show the same recovery in a toast.

**Fade in shape** and **Fade out shape** follow the two fade rows: three buttons, Linear, Equal power and S, with the saved one pressed. Equal power is a quarter sine (a true equal-power pair needs a crossfade). A click saves the shape at once as one `SetClipFade` that keeps both lengths (one Undo step); pressing the saved shape does nothing. At a cut join, or where a blended crossfade replaces the edge, the saved shape stays pressed but the buttons are disabled and say why ("Shape ignored: the join into this clip is a cut"). With several clips selected the row shows their common shape, or no pressed button and "Mixed" when they differ, and a click sets that shape on every selected clip whose edge it applies to in one `SetClipsFade`. A Viewer sees the shape as text. Share-link playback follows the shape at once; the host hears it after the mix rebuilds.

A clip's **Gain** row follows the shape rows. It is a level Scrub field like a track's Volume: -60 to +12 dB in 0.5 dB steps, 0 dB when the clip has no gain, and a reset dot that returns it to 0 dB. Dragging only previews the number; the release, an arrow key or a typed value (Enter or blur) saves one `SetClipGain`, which is one Undo step and carries the saved gain it started from. If Undo or another editor changed the gain first, nothing is saved and the inspector shows "This clip changed. Nothing was saved. Adjust the gain again." With several clips selected the Gain row shows their common level, or "Mixed" when they differ, and a value you choose sets every selected clip to that level in one `SetClipsGain` (one Undo step); the reset dot sets all of them to 0 dB. A Viewer sees the level read-only. Gain is applied before the track's effects and fader. On a share link's proxy playback it is heard at once; on the host the saved mix rebuilds after the edit (Updating mix…), and playback follows the rebuilt mix.

A selected word and an applied edit (a cut) open in the Inspector sheet. The word shows its confidence, Suppressed and Ignored state, the Text and End index fix, Suppress, Ignore and Return to automatic, and its timing; the cut shows its facts with Restore or Open History. A share link sees neither the word fix nor its actions.

On a phone, the Inspector uses Full height when the available room is too short for its dock and fields, then returns to Half when room returns. A manual height change stays selected as the phone size changes.

A selected seam (a cut or a split) opens in the same sheet with a **Length** field, an **Offset** Scrub field and an **A/B** row, in that order so a short landscape sheet keeps Length reachable. Offset is the roll since the sheet opened, in ms to one decimal: drag 1 px is 1 ms, Shift 10 ms, Alt 0.1 ms, and the reset dot returns the seam to where it was when the sheet opened. Its range is the room the clips leave, and release saves one roll and one Undo. A/B plays 1.5 s either side of the seam as it was when the sheet opened (A) and as it is now (B). It needs the host app, so a share link reads "A/B needs the host app"; it reads "Roll the seam to compare" until the seam moves, "A/B is ready once the roll saves" while a roll saves, and "The seam can't return to where it was" when the server clamps the way back. "Preparing the comparison…" is announced while the windows render.

A selected chapter or social clip opens in the same sheet, with no Apply button and no seek buttons (activating the marker in the lane (a click, Enter or Space on it, or choosing it from the +N overflow menu) moves the playhead to it, and selecting it any other way, such as adding a chapter, leaves the playhead where it is; Hear is on the Action bar). A chapter shows **Title** (saved when you press Enter or leave the field; Escape discards an unsaved Title edit. When Title matches the saved value, Escape closes the sheet; a trailing space is trimmed away; an empty title is refused and the field shows the saved title again) and **Time** (drag, arrow keys or type a number; it stays within the episode; each change saves once). Renaming keeps the sheet open on the renamed chapter, and so does an undo, a redo or a collaborator renaming or moving it; if it is removed, or its title and time both change at once, the sheet closes without a message. A chapter is removed with Remove on the Action bar. A social clip shows **Title** (host only; saved on Enter or when you leave the field; an empty title clears it and the clip reads "Untitled clip"), **Start** and **End** (each saves once and shows the allowed range if you type a value outside it), and **Quality**, a five-dot scale for the proposer's score. A positive score below 0.1 shows zero filled dots. A clip added by hand has no Quality row. The minimum clip length is 0.1 s. While a saved clip is shorter than 0.1 s, Start and End allow only edits that lengthen it. Whether a social clip is approved shows on the marker lane: a proposed clip's bracket is hatched. A social clip is removed with Remove on the Action bar. A share link sees a chapter read-only and receives no social clips. While a save runs, Title, Time, Start and End are read-only and marked busy, a drag released in that time saves nothing, and a Title draft left by moving focus away then is dropped and the saved title shows. A failed save stays in the sheet as an alert.

If another editor changes a social clip's saved **Title** while you have a draft, the field keeps your draft and explains the change. Press Enter to save your draft. Choose **Use saved title** or press Escape to show the latest saved title. Moving focus away keeps the draft in the open Inspector without saving it. Use saved title waits while the current save runs. If the save fails, the draft stays and you can press Enter to try again.

While a chapter saves, **Title** and **Time** are read-only and marked busy. A note below them asks you to try again when the save finishes. If the **Time** textbox is open, it keeps focus.

A selected automatic reduction (from the clip's Inspect automatic audio reduction button or its Timeline overlay) opens in the same sheet: the plan, rendered-audio and saved-choice facts, the regions in the clip, **Refresh regions** and **Undo Restore** (Hear before, Hear after and Keep, which restores the original audio, are on the Action bar). A share link reads that the project host alone can inspect and restore it.

A selected comment with no pending edit behind it opens as a comment popover when you press Inspect: a 320 px card in the top layer beside its pin, holding the shared comment card (author, the time that seeks, the comment text, action items, replies and a **Reply** field that sends on Enter). It sits clear of the floating Action bar and hides while the chrome above the lanes would cover it or its pin is out of view (the Action bar instead stays on screen, pinned at the edge with Show edit). Escape or Close dismisses it and returns focus to where you opened it. On a phone the same thread is in the Inspector bottom sheet, and on a tablet holding an agent range it stays in the Inspector card so the range actions keep their place. The Reply field takes focus on open. Escape in a reply with text leaves the field and keeps the text; a second Escape closes. The popover stays mounted, hidden, while its pin is scrolled out of view. Resolve and Remove are on the Action bar; a resolved comment shows **Resolved by** and **Reopen** in the popover or sheet. A share link without reply permission has no Reply field, and a share link never sees Resolve, Reopen or Remove.

A selected suggestion (a pending edit), or a comment on one that is still pending, opens in the same sheet as a compact Decision card: a kind line, the reason, a five-dot confidence meter, Start and End Scrub fields that save as you commit each one (snapping to silence unless Alt is held), the Ask thread (posting the first Ask moves focus to the empty reply field) and, on a sheet with no Action bar, the Current, Suggested and A/B listen controls (Suggested and A/B stay focusable and unavailable when the server gives no Suggested side, with the reason in a note beside them; otherwise Hear before and Hear after are on the bar, Seek to start under More). **Cut** and **Keep** are on the Action bar when the suggestion has a cut, shorten or lower action, **Leave** and **Reject** are there for a suggestion to leave alone (Leave dismisses it and keeps the audio, the same as Backspace, and the toast offers Undo), and **Approve** and **Reject** are there for a split, an exact range proposal with another action, or one with no accept action (an exact range shows them disabled, with the reason in visible text, to anyone the bar reaches but the host or an Editor, which no guest link does today); the sheet then drops its own buttons. For a comment on a pending edit and a phone sheet with no bar dock (the compact drawer, the full sheet at a tight height), the sheet keeps the same buttons (**Cut** and **Keep**, **Leave** and **Reject**, or **Approve** and **Reject**, by the same rule). The phone peek strip's body shows no verbs; the review card docks into its chrome with Cut and Keep (Approve and Reject for the cases above).
