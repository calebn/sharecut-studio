## Shared: Selection → Inspector / Bottom sheet

| | |
|--|--|
| **Purpose** | Modifier for whatever is selected (pending cut, clip fade, track FX, word, …) |
| **Primary actions** | Cut / Keep (Approve / Reject for a split, exact range or no accept action) / Restore · Hear before / Hear after (footer Play around where there is no Action bar) · param edits · Close |
| **Data shown** | Type badge · label · agent reason · source + timeline ranges · shape-specific params |
| **Phone** | Ordinary modifier sheets use half or full height. Heading, actions, fields, errors, and audition footer scroll together. Scroll to the top for Close, Expand, and Collapse. Ordinary modifiers use a taller half height. The compact inspector uses peek, half, and full heights with its header pinned outside one bounded body scroller. Feedback and fields scroll in that body. A clip's trim and fade fields, a suggestion's Source start and end, and an envelope point's Time and Level each carry the strip's fine and coarse minus and plus nudge buttons (hold to repeat) beside the field. Sheets never stack. |
| **Tablet** | The Inspector card anchors to the floating Action bar in the Edit workspace, with the selected element or workspace bottom edge as fallback. Its heading stays fixed while its body scrolls. |
| **Desktop** | The Inspector card anchors to the floating Action bar in the Edit workspace, with the selected element or workspace bottom edge as fallback. Its heading stays fixed while its body scrolls. |
| **Focus** | Close returns focus to the control that opened the Inspector, including a phone sheet opened from a track header. Focus you moved elsewhere before Close stays where it is. |
| **Empty** | “Select a clip, edit, or word” |
| **Out of scope** | Deep nested wizards; one panel per MCP tool name |

A fade gesture based on values changed by Undo or another editor saves nothing.
The current project shows "This clip changed. Nothing was saved. Adjust the fade
again." Repeat the gesture against the restored values. The inspector shows the
error inline; timeline handles and phone nudges show the same recovery in a toast.

**Fade in shape** and **Fade out shape** follow the two fade rows: three buttons, Linear, Equal power and S, with the saved one pressed. Equal power is a quarter sine (a true equal-power pair needs a crossfade). A click saves the shape at once as one `SetClipFade` that keeps both lengths (one Undo step); pressing the saved shape does nothing. At a cut join, or where a blended crossfade replaces the edge, the saved shape stays pressed but the buttons are disabled and say why ("Shape ignored: the join into this clip is a cut"). With several clips selected the row shows their common shape, or no pressed button and "Mixed" when they differ, and a click sets that shape on every selected clip whose edge it applies to in one `SetClipsFade`. A Viewer sees the shape as text. Share-link playback follows the shape at once; the host hears it after the mix rebuilds.

A clip's **Gain** row follows the shape rows. It is a level Scrub field like a track's Volume: -60 to +12 dB in 0.5 dB steps, 0 dB when the clip has no gain, and a reset dot that returns it to 0 dB. Dragging only previews the number; the release, an arrow key or a typed value (Enter or blur) saves one `SetClipGain`, which is one Undo step and carries the saved gain it started from. If Undo or another editor changed the gain first, nothing is saved and the inspector shows "This clip changed. Nothing was saved. Adjust the gain again." With several clips selected the Gain row shows their common level, or "Mixed" when they differ, and a value you choose sets every selected clip to that level in one `SetClipsGain` (one Undo step); the reset dot sets all of them to 0 dB. A Viewer sees the level read-only. Gain is applied before the track's effects and fader. On a share link's proxy playback it is heard at once; on the host the saved mix rebuilds after the edit (Updating mix…), and playback follows the rebuilt mix.

A selected word and an applied edit (a cut) open in the Inspector sheet. The word shows its confidence, Suppressed and Ignored state, the Text and End index fix, Suppress, Ignore and Return to automatic, and its timing; the cut shows its facts with Restore or Open History. A share link sees neither the word fix nor its actions.

On a phone, the Inspector uses Full height when the available room is too short for its dock and fields, then returns to Half when room returns. A manual height change stays selected as the phone size changes.

A selected seam (a cut or a split) opens in the same sheet with a **Length** field, an **Offset** Scrub field and an **A/B** row, in that order so a short landscape sheet keeps Length reachable. Offset is the roll since the sheet opened, in ms to one decimal: drag 1 px is 1 ms, Shift 10 ms, Alt 0.1 ms, and the reset dot returns the seam to where it was when the sheet opened. Its range is the room the clips leave, and release saves one roll and one Undo. A/B plays 1.5 s either side of the seam as it was when the sheet opened (A) and as it is now (B). It needs the host app, so a share link reads "A/B needs the host app"; it reads "Roll the seam to compare" until the seam moves, "A/B is ready once the roll saves" while a roll saves, and "The seam can't return to where it was" when the server clamps the way back. "Preparing the comparison…" is announced while the windows render.

A selected chapter or social clip opens in the same sheet, with no Apply button and no seek buttons (activating the marker in the lane (a click, Enter or Space on it, or choosing it from the +N overflow menu) moves the playhead to it, and selecting it any other way, such as adding a chapter, leaves the playhead where it is; Hear is on the Action bar). A chapter shows **Title** (saved when you press Enter or leave the field; Escape puts the title back and a second Escape closes the sheet; a trailing space is trimmed away; an empty title is refused and the field shows the saved title again) and **Time** (drag, arrow keys or type a number; it stays within the episode; each change saves once). Renaming keeps the sheet open on the renamed chapter, and so does an undo, a redo or a collaborator renaming or moving it; if it is removed, or its title and time both change at once, the sheet closes without a message. A chapter is removed with Remove on the Action bar. A social clip shows **Title** (host only; saved on Enter or when you leave the field; an empty title clears it and the clip reads "Untitled clip"), **Start** and **End** (each saves once; Start stays at least 0.1 s before End, and a typed value outside the range is refused with the range), and **Quality**, five dots from the proposer's score (a clip added by hand has no Quality row). Whether a social clip is approved shows on the marker lane: a proposed clip's bracket is hatched. A social clip is removed with Remove on the Action bar. A share link sees a chapter read-only and receives no social clips. While a save runs, Title, Time, Start and End are read-only and marked busy, a drag released in that time saves nothing, and a Title draft left by moving focus away then is dropped and the saved title shows. A failed save stays in the sheet as an alert.

A selected automatic reduction (from the clip's Inspect automatic audio reduction button or its Timeline overlay) opens in the same sheet: the plan, rendered-audio and saved-choice facts, the regions in the clip, **Refresh regions** and **Undo Restore** (Hear before, Hear after and Keep, which restores the original audio, are on the Action bar). A share link reads that the project host alone can inspect and restore it.

A selected comment with no pending edit behind it opens as a comment popover when you press Inspect: a 320 px card in the top layer beside its pin, holding the shared comment card (author, the time that seeks, the comment text, action items, replies and a **Reply** field that sends on Enter). It sits clear of the floating Action bar and hides, as the bar does, while the chrome above the lanes would cover it. Escape or Close dismisses it and returns focus to where you opened it. On a phone the same thread is in the Inspector bottom sheet, and on a tablet holding an agent range it stays in the Inspector card so the range actions keep their place. The Reply field takes focus on open. Escape in a reply with text leaves the field and keeps the text; a second Escape closes. The popover stays mounted, hidden, while its pin is scrolled out of view. Resolve and Remove are on the Action bar; a resolved comment shows **Resolved by** and **Reopen** in the popover or sheet. A share link without reply permission has no Reply field, and a share link never sees Resolve, Reopen or Remove.

A selected suggestion (a pending edit), or a comment on one that is still pending, opens in the same sheet as a compact Decision card: a kind line, the reason, a five-dot confidence meter, Start and End Scrub fields that save as you commit each one (snapping to silence unless Alt is held), the Ask thread (posting the first Ask moves focus to the empty reply field) and, on a sheet with no Action bar, the Current, Suggested and A/B listen controls (Suggested and A/B stay focusable and unavailable when the server gives no Suggested side, with the reason in a note beside them; otherwise Hear before and Hear after are on the bar, Seek to start under More). **Cut** and **Keep** are on the Action bar when the suggestion has an accept action, and the sheet then drops its own buttons; for a suggestion with no accept action, a split, an exact range proposal, a comment on a pending edit and a phone sheet with no bar dock (the compact drawer, the full sheet at a tight height), **Approve** and **Reject** stay in the sheet. The phone peek strip's body shows no verbs; the review card docks into its chrome with Cut and Keep (Approve and Reject for the cases above).
