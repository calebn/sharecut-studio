## Transcript export (Deliver)

The Transcript section offers **Text**, **Markdown**, **Reading copy**, **SRT**, and **VTT**. Text keeps the timestamped detailed TXT export. Reading copy writes a paragraph-style `readable.txt` with adjacent speaker turns grouped under one header. It appears in Transcript files with its own name and current or stale state. Export again refreshes a stale reading copy. Audio export does not refresh it. The existing defaults, preview, and Copy output stay unchanged.
