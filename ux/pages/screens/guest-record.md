## Guest / record (`/rec/{token}`) (Join sheet + full-quality recording + mix-minus + landing + live comments shipped)

Join sheet, consent, people tiles, status pill (live, paused, saving), full-quality recording on each
participant's device, mix-minus monitor, saving to the project, timeline
landing, and live comments ship.
Spec: [recording-session.md](https://github.com/calebn/sharecut-studio/blob/main/docs/recording-session.md).
Journeys: [Guest journeys § 6–7](#/journeys).

### Join sheet: where it departs from the record spec (issue 3834)

1. The spec asks for a phone bottom sheet at a large detent (88%). The sheet has no detents: it fits its rows and stops at 88% of the screen. The room still shows above it.
2. The spec's "phone under 600" now holds for the Join sheet only. The room and the Device sheet keep the 767 viewport class.
3. "Not on phone" for the name field's focus is read as "not on a touch pointer".
4. Focus goes to the control that unblocks the primary button, and to the primary only when it is enabled. A disabled button cannot take focus.
5. **Allow microphone** stays a full-width secondary button at 44 px, so the sheet keeps one primary.
6. The card uses the shared centred-card width (400 px), not the spec's 420 px.
7. The back gesture leaves the page, as before.
8. The Device sheet's **Retry** is a secondary button now, not primary, so each sheet keeps one primary.
