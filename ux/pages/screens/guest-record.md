## Guest / record (`/rec/{token}`) (Join sheet + full-quality recording + mix-minus + landing + live comments shipped)

Join sheet, consent, people tiles, status pill (live, paused, saving), full-quality recording on each
participant's device, mix-minus monitor, saving to the project, timeline
landing, and live comments ship.
Spec: [recording-session.md](https://github.com/calebn/sharecut-studio/blob/main/docs/recording-session.md).
Journeys: [Guest journeys § 6–7](#/journeys).

| Surface | Who | What they see |
|---------|-----|----------------|
| **Loading** | Guest and producer | While the link is looked up, the Room shell shows at once, with no blank screen and no "Loading studio…": the wordmark, a grey bar for the title, four grey placeholder tiles (a 2x2 grid on a phone), and the dock dimmed and out of reach. Nothing moves. After 4 seconds the alert slot adds **Still connecting…** with **Retry**, which looks the room up again and restarts the 4 second count. The page is marked busy and carries the hidden text "Joining the room…" for a screen reader to find; the only spoken announcement is "Still connecting…" at 4 seconds. Focus is never trapped; Retry is the first control you can reach, and after you press it focus moves to the page title. A link that cannot be found opens the invalid-link cover instead. |
| **Join sheet** | Guest (recorded) | Over the shared room: name, microphone (Allow microphone, input chip that opens a menu of inputs, own level), storage check, headphones toggle, then **Join and record** (joining is consent; **Details** carries the notice). Wait for host Start. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Device sheet** | Recorded guest and host | Opens from your own tile or More ▸ Devices: input chip and menu (Default first, the current one checked; disabled with the reason during a take) with level meter, headphones toggle (guests), **Speaker** menu, **Record room tone**. The Speaker menu appears only where the browser can pick where the room plays (where `AudioContext.setSinkId` exists); elsewhere it is absent, not disabled. A pick applies at once, is remembered in this browser, and the room plays on Default, with a note, while the speaker is missing or will not open; the pick returns when it does. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Join sheet** | Producer | Name and **Join to listen**; listed as listening and not recorded; no mic, no consent. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Join sheet / room / Record stage** | Recorded guest and host | **Own** peak meter (sample peak only) with a clip indicator; a clipping badge on the own tile (the host's room tiles also show each guest's live level and, once their segments upload, their clip count for the take) and, once the third clip of the take starts within 30 s of the first of those three, the alert "You're clipping. Move back a little."; the host's Takes tray jumps to the first clipped span once the take lands. Producers and the Full room Cover have none. |
| **Record room** | Guest | Status pill (`m:ss · Take N`, `Paused · m:ss`, `Saving · N%`, `Saved`); mute (silence in the full-quality recording + send stops); a tile per person (ringed in that person's colour; the host's tile has a small Record glyph and reads "host" after the name, for every seat); notes rail (their own notes and every marker; other people's notes too when the host turns on Guests see notes); "Recording in full quality on this device." and **Hearing the room.** as screen-reader lines; then host timeline clips. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Record stage** | Host | Opened by Home's Record on a new project, it creates the first room at once and opens the Invite sheet at Record links (from later visits, the empty state still asks: **Create record room** or **Start Session N**). Start / Pause / Resume / Stop (hold the Record button, or tap Stop take beside Resume on a paused take); a status line per recorded participant and segment ("Saving to project…", then "Saved to project"). A saved file auto-lands its clips; a failed land shows **Retry** on the take row in the Takes tray. Storage, saving, keeper and monitor failures show once, in the Alert slot (no second paragraph above the room); "Recording in full quality on this device." and "Hearing the room." are screen-reader lines only. **More** has no Stop take or Land. **More** has **End session**: a toast with **Undo** stays up for 10 s before the links stop working. The Notes field is held with "Notes open while a take is running." outside a running take (as on the guest page), and a note that cannot be sent keeps its text in the field. Every other person's tile has an options menu with **Remove from room**, which asks "Remove {name} from the room?" first. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Record room** | Producer | Same pill and tiles, listed as not recorded; mix-minus of recorded peers; no full-quality recording, no saving status. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Full room** | 5th recorded or 3rd producer | Cover "The room is full" with **Try again**; never `getUserMedia`. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Declined** | Guest who chose Not now | The room dims under "You're not being recorded." with **Join recording** (works while the room waits for a take); host may re-invite as producer. **Shipped (no screenshot yet).** |
| **Host offline** | Recorded guest | Alert "{Host} dropped. You're still recording on this device." The full-quality recording stays open. **Shipped with full-quality recording.** |
| **Native close confirmation** | Host or recorded guest in the desktop app | During REC, PAUSED, or finalizing, closing the native window asks for confirmation. Host copy warns that the session stops for everyone; guest copy warns about the guest's full-quality recording. This is local desktop protection and sends no remote close command. The macOS app menu and **Cmd+Q** use the confirmation path; Dock **Quit** and OS shutdown remain best-effort. |

Prefix `/rec/` with a review token opens the recording invalid-link cover: “This recording link is invalid or has ended. Ask the host for a new link.” A record token on `/r/` opens the review cover: “This link is invalid or has ended. Ask the host for a new link.”
