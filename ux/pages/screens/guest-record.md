## Guest / record (`/rec/{token}`) (Join sheet + full-quality recording + mix-minus + landing + live comments shipped)

Join sheet, consent, people tiles, status pill (live, paused, saving), full-quality recording on each
participant's device, mix-minus monitor, saving to the project, timeline
landing, and live comments ship.
Spec: [recording-session.md](https://github.com/calebn/sharecut-studio/blob/main/docs/recording-session.md).
Journeys: [Guest journeys § 6–7](#/journeys).

### Join sheet: where it departs from the record spec (issue 3834)

1. The spec says the phone sheet is Full at 92% (spec-record.md:4, design-system.md:740 L19), and spec-record.md:132 and :348 say a large detent at 88%. The sheet has no detents: it fits its rows and stops at 88% of the screen. The room still shows above it.
2. The spec's "phone under 600" now holds for the Join sheet only. The room and the Device sheet keep the 767 viewport class.
3. "Not on phone" for the name field's focus is read as "not on a touch pointer".
4. Focus goes to the control that unblocks the primary button, and to the primary only when it is enabled. A disabled button cannot take focus.
5. **Allow microphone** stays a full-width secondary button at 44 px, so the sheet keeps one primary.
6. The card uses the shared centred-card width (400 px), not the spec's 420 px.
7. Headphones are "Required to join" (spec-record.md:136) on a laptop or tablet only. On a phone the row is a two-answer choice (Q6, spec-record.md:379, decided by the owner), and either answer joins. Q6's premise, that the phone earpiece and echo cancellation make speaker use workable, does not hold: the page asks for no echo cancellation.
8. The spec's preset from a connected headphone device (spec-record.md:136) is not built on any device: a connected headset is not a worn one.
9. The Device sheet's "headphone toggle" (spec-record.md:213 and :350) is the same two side-by-side buttons on a phone.
10. The person tile's glyph cluster gains a crossed-out headphones glyph (spec-record.md:338), for a guest who said they are not wearing headphones.
11. "Phone" for the headphones rule is a device test (coarse primary pointer, screen short side under 600 px), not the Join sheet's 600 container query or the 767 layout class.
12. The back gesture leaves the page, as before.
13. The Device sheet's **Retry** is a secondary button now, not primary, so each sheet keeps one primary.
14. The card is not a fixed 96 px from the top: its top is 12% of the screen height, at least 12 px and at most 96 px, and 8 px when the sheet scrolls as one.

### Record room on a portrait phone: where it departs from the record spec (issue 3834)

1. The portrait phone header shows the ShareCut symbol, not the wordmark (spec-record.md:89, :171-172). The name "ShareCut" stays in the page as text. A landscape phone keeps the wordmark.
2. The portrait phone title may take two lines inside the 44 px header.
3. On a portrait phone (320 to 767 wide) the guest dock (Mute or Unmute, Comment while a take runs or is paused, Leave; Done once saved) and the producer dock (Comment while a take runs or is paused, Leave) label each control with a word, so the dock is taller than the spec's 88 px: 101 px while waiting, live and paused, for a guest and a producer alike and at every width in that range (spec-record.md:172, :340; design-system.md:171). A producer's waiting dock holds only Leave and is the same height, so it does not jump when the take starts, and the Saved dock keeps that height. A guest's dock is taller while saving, at every width in the range, because the "Saving · N% · keep this page open" line sits under the one Leave button; a producer has no saving step (the pill reads Saved at once), so a producer's dock stays that height. A bottom safe-area inset can make any of them taller still. Icons still carry the meaning; the words are an addition. A landscape phone's dock is unchanged and icon only.
4. The portrait phone guest and producer docks drop the Rail button; the Comments strip is the one door to the list (design-system.md:171). The host studio's dock, and a landscape phone's, a tablet's and a laptop's, keep the toggle.
5. The own tile reads "(you)" and shows a Devices cue on the guest page at every width except a landscape phone up to 767 px wide, where the tile reads "(you)" without the cue (spec-record.md:338). The host studio's own tile is unchanged.
6. "Phone" for these rules is the portrait 767 layout class, including portrait widths from 600 to 767 (spec-record.md:172 says under 600), as Join sheet departure 2 already says.
7. On every guest-page tile except those of a landscape phone up to 767 px wide (so on a portrait phone, a tablet, a laptop and a wider landscape phone, with the Comments rail open or closed) the avatar area starts 20 px lower than the spec's tile. That keeps the Devices cue clear of the avatar and of its full level ring and keeps the avatars level with each other; the own tile's level meter still makes its avatar sit a few pixels higher than the others, as before. The cue is 16 px high. The host studio's tiles and those of a landscape phone up to 767 px wide keep the spec's position.

### Looking in: where it departs from the record spec (issue 3834; inert until a server sends `watchers`)

1. The spec's roster (CMP-REC-ROSTER, spec-record.md:235-238) ends at the listener row. When a count is sent, a nameless looking-in line sits on the host, guest and producer rosters above the tiles and under the header, not after the listener row, so a short window cannot push it out of view.
2. When a count is sent, the guest's consent **Details** (SCR-REC-LOBBY-GUEST) names who is looking in and says they cannot hear the room.
3. The spec has no live region for a roster change. When a count is sent, the host, guest and producer pages announce the first looking-in arrival in each room, while the room is waiting or stopped only, never during a take or the room-tone countdown.
